## Advanced Retail Sales Analysis with Python

A complete retail sales analysis project using Python to explore sales performance, profitability, discounts, product categories, and yearly trends.

### Project Overview

I worked with a retail sales dataset to build a structured data analysis workflow, starting from data inspection and cleaning and progressing to feature engineering, KPI analysis, and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).

The analysis focused on understanding not only sales volume, but also profitability and the factors that may affect business performance.

### Workflow

* Data Import & Initial Inspection
* Data Cleaning & Preparation
* Feature Engineering
* KPI Analysis
* Category & Sub-Category Analysis
* Discount & Profitability Analysis
* Yearly Trend Analysis
* Business Insights & Recommendations

### Key Results

* **Total Sales:** $2.30M
* **Total Profit:** $286.40K
* **Total Quantity:** 37,873 units
* **Overall Profit Margin:** 12.47%

### Key Insights

* **Technology** generated the highest profit among the three main categories, with a profit margin of approximately **17.40%**.
* **Furniture** generated significant sales but had a much lower profit margin of approximately **2.49%**, indicating a profitability issue despite its sales volume.
* **Tables** was one of the major loss-making sub-categories and contributed significantly to Furniture's weak profitability.
* Higher discount levels were associated with lower profitability, especially at aggressive discount levels.
* The analysis of **Tables** showed that profitability deteriorated as discounts increased, with the sub-category becoming unprofitable around the **20% discount level**.
* Total sales and total profit generally increased from **2016 to 2019**, while profit margin varied across the years.

### Business Recommendations

* Review the discount strategy for loss-making products, especially Tables.
* Investigate pricing, cost, and discount structures for low-margin sub-categories.
* Evaluate performance using **profit margin alongside sales**, rather than relying on sales volume alone.
* Continue the analysis across customers, regions, shipping performance, correlations, and distributions.
